Seven yokai. One human festival. Team up with 2–4 players to hide your true form, charm guests in five-second bursts, win six stall games and face the Demon King. One player must escort three guests to the summit before time runs out. Building it taught me how to guide AI through 3D character animation, synchronized multiplayer and debugging across screen sizes. The fun is covering a friend just as their disguise slips.
