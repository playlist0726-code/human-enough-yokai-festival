Human Enough!

Approach a stall and press E to play a first-person minigame and win a prize.

WASD to move · Space to jump · Mouse wheel to zoom · Right-drag to look in any direction, including the sky. Use Mouse look for hands-free camera control; Esc releases it. On touchscreens, drag the world to look and pinch to zoom.

Collect glowing potions and drink them to restore your spirit.

Press F near a human to make them follow for 5 seconds. They stop and wait when it ends. Charm them again to continue uphill.

Each charm costs spirit. Collect and drink potions to keep guiding your guests.

You enter as a human. Every 15 seconds, a yokai feature shows for 5 seconds. Press C near a teammate to hide their features for 5 seconds. Cover does not extend a charm.

Stay close to a teammate: R shares one potion; T hands over one following guest. The original 5-second timer keeps running. Deliveries count for the new escort.

G calls for help and marks your location on everyone’s map. Choose a teammate in the crew menu to direct your potion shares and handoffs.

The Demon King invades the festival. Dodge orange circles, jump over purple shockwaves, or press C to protect your crew with a talisman ward. Press E beside purple seals and stay close until they break. When his crystal opens, hold J / Spirit bolt to attack. Alternate shots with teammates for bonus damage. Press E near a sealed teammate to rescue them. Potions also restore one heart. Banishing the king rewards your crew with time, potions and a protected sprint.

Win together: one player must bring all 3 humans to the summit shrine. Team totals do not combine. The round starts with 8 minutes; banishing the Demon King can add time.

Lose: time runs out, or every connected multiplayer teammate remains sealed for 4 seconds. A solo player reforms after 8 seconds. Stay close, ward with C, and rescue with E.

Esc or ☰ opens your menu, settings and stall journal. Solo play pauses; online play keeps moving.
